Common Questions

Looking for a quick answer? Start here.

How do I get started?
Start by reviewing the official Pennsylvania Lottery website and registration requirements. If you are using Richardson's approved referral, use the referral button on this page.
How old do I have to be?
You must meet the Pennsylvania Lottery's applicable age requirement for online play. The current program information states that online play is for people 18 and older.
Can I play online from outside Pennsylvania?
No. Pennsylvania Lottery online play requires you to be physically located in Pennsylvania when playing online.
Is this the official Pennsylvania Lottery website?
No. This is Richardson's personal referral page. It is not operated by or presented as the official Pennsylvania Lottery. Use the Official PA Lottery button to visit the official site.
What if I already have a PA Lottery account?
Referral eligibility can depend on whether a person is already registered or associated with another referral source. If you already have an account, review the official program terms before assuming a referral will apply.
Where can I find official rules?
Use the Official PA Lottery button on this page to access the Pennsylvania Lottery's official website and current rules.
